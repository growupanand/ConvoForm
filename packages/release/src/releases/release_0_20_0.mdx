## What's new

**This update connects ConvoForm to the tools you already use and shows you exactly what happened in every conversation.** 🚀

We've focused on getting your responses where they need to go, and on making sure no answer gets lost along the way.

### 🔗 Webhooks & Integrations

![Webhook delivery details on a conversation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/growupanand/ConvoForm/release-0.20.0-screenshots/release-0.20.0/webhook-delivery.png)

Send every completed response straight to your own systems.

- **Outgoing Webhooks**: Add a URL and ConvoForm sends each completed response to it as a signed request. Failed deliveries are retried automatically.
- **Delivery Log & Test**: See every delivery and its status in settings, and send a test event before going live.
- **Google Sheets Sync**: Sync form responses into a Google Sheet automatically.
- **Telegram Channel**: Run your form as a conversation on Telegram.

### 🧭 Conversation Journey

![Conversation journey timeline](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/growupanand/ConvoForm/release-0.20.0-screenshots/release-0.20.0/conversation-journey.png)

Every conversation now shows a simple timeline at the top: when it started, how many fields were answered, whether it finished or where it was abandoned, and whether insights and webhooks went out. Click the webhook step to see delivery details.

### 📱 Easier Getting Started

- **Sign Up on Your Phone**: Coming from WhatsApp or Instagram? You can now sign up and sign in on mobile, and we'll email you a link to keep building on your laptop.
- **Instant Workspace**: New accounts get a personal workspace automatically and go straight to creating their first form.

### ⚡ Faster & More Reliable

- **Live Updates on Cloudflare**: Live responses and conversation updates now run on Cloudflare Workers for faster, steadier real-time updates.
- **Reliable Saving**: Every answer and completion is now saved reliably, even when many messages arrive close together.
- **Smarter Insights**: AI insights are generated on the server as soon as a conversation completes.
- **Better Validation**: Rating answers outside the allowed range are rejected.
- **Security Hardening**: Email and integration sync endpoints now require internal authentication.

### 🌐 A New ConvoForm.com

A fully redesigned website with a live demo, a new logo, dedicated pages for study-abroad agencies and job applications, and new guides in the articles section.
