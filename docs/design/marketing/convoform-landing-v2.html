What ConvoForm doesn't do

  • It doesn't recommend universities or judge visa eligibility. Your team does that.
  • It doesn't rank or qualify people. It checks that each answer meets the field.
  • It isn't a WhatsApp bot. People open your link and chat in the browser.
  • It doesn't replace your CRM, book calls or send follow-ups.

FAQ

Questions, answered

01Is it free?
Yes, you can create forms and collect responses for free with no credit card. [confirm: any limits on the cloud plan, and whether paid plans are planned]
02Can I self-host?
Yes. ConvoForm is open source (Apache 2.0). Follow the self-hosting guide on docs.convoform.com to run it with Docker Compose and your own PostgreSQL database and AI provider key.
03Does it work on WhatsApp and mobile?
Share your form link anywhere, including WhatsApp and your Instagram bio. People tap it and chat in their phone's browser. ConvoForm doesn't run inside WhatsApp and doesn't send WhatsApp messages.
04Where is my data stored?
On the cloud version, forms and responses are stored in a PostgreSQL database run by ConvoForm, and answers are processed by an AI model to run the conversation. If you self-host, everything stays on your own server and database. [confirm: cloud database region and which AI provider is named]
05Can I import my existing Google Form?
Yes. Sign in, then import a Google Form from your Google Drive to turn it into a conversational form.
